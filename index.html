Message from the District Supervisor
MS

Marianne M. Sabod

Public Schools District Supervisor · Tboli East District

Warm greetings from the highlands of Tboli!

This portal answers a question our own research asked of us. In our BERF 2025 study, we found that of the 17 action research studies completed across the district, only four ever reached an Annual Implementation Plan. Excellent work by our teachers was being shelved — not for lack of quality, but for lack of a system.

The Research Utilization Portal is that system. Here, every completed study is preserved, searchable and connected to the policies, classroom practices and school plans it informs. I invite every teacher, school head and partner to use it — and to keep asking the questions that make our schools better for T'boli learners.
